ffmpeg-core.wasm — FFmpeg

@ffmpeg/core 0.12.10, the WebAssembly build of FFmpeg n5.1.4 that does the actual encoding, is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. It is built with --enable-gpl together with libx264 and libx265, which makes the GPL, rather than the LGPL, the governing license for the binary.

The binary shipped here is vendor/core/ffmpeg-core.wasm. Its license texts are at vendor/core/COPYING.GPLv2 and vendor/core/FFMPEG-LICENSE.md.

Corresponding source. The complete corresponding source for this binary, and the scripts used to build it, are:

  • FFmpeg n5.1.4 — github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg at tag n5.1.4
  • The Emscripten build configuration — github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm at v0.12.15 (Dockerfile, build/ffmpeg.sh, build/ffmpeg-wasm.sh)

Libraries compiled into that build: libx264, libx265, libvpx, libmp3lame, libtheora, libvorbis, libopus, zlib, libwebp, libfreetype, libfribidi, libass and libzimg, each under its own license as documented by its upstream project.

What ReCoding actually uses
MP3
libmp3lame (LGPL-2.0-or-later)
AAC-LC
FFmpeg's native aac encoder (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
Opus
libopus (BSD-3-Clause)
FLAC
FFmpeg's native flac encoder (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
ALAC
FFmpeg's native alac encoder (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
WAV
FFmpeg's native pcm_*le encoders (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
AIFF
FFmpeg's native pcm_*be encoders (LGPL-2.1-or-later)

HE-AAC and HE-AAC v2 are deliberately absent. They require the libfdk_aac encoder, which FFmpeg can only be built against with --enable-nonfree — and the resulting binary may not be redistributed. AAC here is therefore AAC-LC only.
